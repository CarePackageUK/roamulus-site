FAQ

Good to know.

Anything else? Visit support or email hello@roamulus.com.

Does Roamulus plan around my ferry?

Yes, with Pro. Tell Plan it for me which Channel crossing you’ve booked, or leave it blank and it picks one. Each day’s stops stay on the right side of the water, and the crossing goes into your trip budget.

Does it work offline?

Your trips, van records and the emergency numbers are kept on your phone, so they’re there with no signal. Maps, searches and weather need a connection, so download offline maps in Apple Maps before you go.

Do I need an account?

No. You can plan locally, sync your records through iCloud and choose which trips to share with a partner. There are no ads. Read the privacy policy.

Which devices can I use?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. An Android version is in development: email us to hear when it’s ready.

Why is it called Roamulus?

It’s Romulus, the founder of Rome, with a little more roam in him. The Romans made camp at the end of every day’s march. Roamulus finds yours.
