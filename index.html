FAQ

Good
to know.

Anything else? Visit support or email hello@roamulus.com.

Does Roamulus plan around my ferry?

Yes, with Pro. Tell Plan it for me which crossing you’ve booked, or leave it blank and it picks one. Each day’s stops stay on the right side of the water.

Will it warn me if my van won’t fit?

Add your van’s height and length. Roamulus warns you about low barriers near your stops in Britain and Ireland, and campsites too small for your van. Always check signs and access before you arrive.

Does it work offline?

Your trips, van records and the emergency numbers are kept on your phone, so they’re there with no signal. With Pro you can save a trip for no signal. Maps, search and weather need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no account and no ads. Your trips stay on your phone, with optional iCloud sync. Read the privacy policy.

Is Roamulus on Android?

Not yet. It’s for iPhone running iOS 17 or later today, and an Android version is in the works. Email us to hear when it’s ready.

Why is it called Roamulus?

It’s Romulus, the founder of Rome, with a little more roam in him. In the legend a she-wolf called Lupa looked after him. In the app, Lupa keeps watch over your trips and your van.
