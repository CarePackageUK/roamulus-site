Your trips and your phone

Roamulus works without a Roamulus account. Your phone stores the records you enter, including your first name, vehicle and renewal details, places, trip dates and stops, diary, packing and shopping lists, fuel and other expenses. Saved travel plans can reveal where you live or expect to be. The profile picture you choose stays outside the app’s trip-sharing and sync records.

You can edit or remove records in the app. Deleting the app can remove its local records. Device backups, exported files, and copies on other devices have their own lifetimes. A widget or notification can show trip or van details to someone looking at your screen. You choose widgets and notification previews in iOS.

Location, searches and forecasts

With your permission, Roamulus uses your location while you use the app. Apple Maps handles map display, searches, place matching and driving directions. Those requests may include a search term, map area, starting point and stop coordinates. A loop that starts where you are can save that location as a trip stop. To plan without providing your current position, search for a place or enter a starting point.

Apple Weather receives rounded coordinates for local weather or a forecast for a trip stop. Results can be cached to avoid repeat requests. You can change location access and precision in iOS Settings. Emergency numbers are bundled with the app and can be looked up offline. Tapping to call uses your phone service.

Telling Lupa about a trip

In versions that include Tell Lupa, tapping its microphone asks for microphone and speech recognition access. The app recognises speech on your iPhone and offers typing when on-device recognition is unavailable. Roamulus does not send the recording or full transcript to its developer or a cloud AI service. Place names from your request may be looked up with Apple Maps. Details you choose to put in a trip become ordinary trip records.

You can turn off microphone and speech recognition access in iOS Settings. If you invoke the app through Siri, Apple processes the Siri interaction under your Siri settings.

Campsites and community searches

Roamulus searches the bundled OpenStreetMap and OpenCampingMap campsite and facility data on your phone. Some place names and touring data come from GeoNames. Online maps, supplementary Apple Maps searches and traveller-suggested spots have different data flows. To find approved community spots near an area, the app sends the search centre and radius to Apple’s CloudKit service. The centre may be near your present location if you selected it.

Suggesting, reporting or removing a spot

Suggesting or reporting requires iCloud sign-in. Roamulus sends the pin, name, type, price, stay limits, facilities and notes you submit to the app’s community database hosted by Apple. CloudKit associates a submission or report with a pseudonymous creator identifier. The moderator can review submissions and reports. Approval makes the spot’s details and exact pin available to other travellers. A report records the spot and the reason you select.

Only submit places where overnight stays are allowed. Keep private addresses, personal contact details and information about other people out of your notes. Use Report a problem on a published spot or email the spot’s name and approximate location to request correction or removal. Hiding a spot on your phone affects only your own view.

Moving from Vanlife Companion

Roamulus can read a Vanlife Companion backup on your device. If you previously used private iCloud sync, turning on the corresponding sync feature in Roamulus can retrieve existing records from the same Apple iCloud container. An App Store purchase and an exported trip backup are separate.

Photos and camera

The trip diary stores references to photos you select from your library. With your permission, it groups them in a trip album in Photos. Taking a diary photo saves the image in your library. Removing an item from the diary leaves the library original in place. Use Photos to manage originals and iCloud Photos to control Apple’s separate photo sync. Diary image files are not included in the trip record sent to a partner.

You can change camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings.

Private iCloud sync and sharing with a partner

Private sync is optional. If you enable it, Apple CloudKit stores a copy of your app records in the private database for your Apple Account. This includes trip and van records, your first name, expenses and lists. Roamulus support cannot retrieve that private database for you.

Inviting a partner shares the trips you select. Selected trips can contain exact stops, dates, diary entries, expenses and packing lists. You can also choose to share the shopping list, which both participants can edit. The rest of your private trips and your garage stay outside the invitation. Apple handles invitations and participant identities.

Manage invitations in Profile → iCloud Sync & Sharing. Stopping sync leaves existing copies in place. Remove from iCloud deletes the owner’s app records from iCloud. Ending a share stops future updates, but participants may retain local copies or exports.

Website icons, map links and handoffs

When a place card requests an icon, the place’s website may receive your IP address and normal connection information. The icon request does not include trip coordinates. When you open a place website or choose Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, the destination handles the information you send. A day route can include multiple stops.

In versions with spot import, resolving a shortened map link contacts the link provider and can follow a redirect. The provider receives the requested URL and connection information. GPX files are read on your device. Roamulus may ask Apple to identify the town or country for imported coordinates.

Backups and exports

A backup is a readable JSON file containing your app records and regional preferences. A trip export or keepsake can contain stops, travel dates or notes. The app passes your selection to the destination you choose in the share or file picker. Check it before sending, especially if a route begins at home. Anyone with access to an exported backup may be able to read it. Delete exported copies separately where you saved or sent them.

UK registration lookup

If you enter a registration and request a lookup, Roamulus sends that registration to its Cloudflare Worker. The Worker queries the DVSA MOT history service and selects vehicle details, including make, model, manufacture year, engine size, fuel type, colour, current MOT expiry and the latest available MOT mileage, date and unit. You choose what to apply to your van record and can enter details manually instead.

Cloudflare processes the connection and the service uses the client IP address to limit repeated requests. The lookup code processes DVSA’s response in memory and does not maintain a search history. Infrastructure providers may also process technical data for security. Details saved to a van follow your private sync and backup choices.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles App Store purchases, renewals and refunds. Roamulus checks Apple-verified transaction and entitlement details to unlock Pro. We do not receive your card or bank details. Apple may make sales reports and diagnostic information, such as crash reports, available to the developer under its own developer services and the analytics-sharing settings on your device. We use diagnostics we receive to identify and fix faults.

Support email

If you email hello@roamulus.com, we receive your address, message, attachments and correspondence history. Incoming mail is routed through Spacemail. We use the information to reply, investigate the issue and deal with any resulting complaint or claim. Send only what is needed to explain the problem. Please leave out passwords, full backups, payment details and other people’s contact details.

This website and its providers

GitHub Pages hosts roamulus.com. GitHub receives IP addresses and technical request information when it serves or secures a page. The site has no web forms, third-party advertising, visitor accounts or analytics cookies. Email links open your email application. Roamulus does not sell personal information or track people across apps for advertising.

Our services include providers that operate internationally, including in the United States. Safeguards vary by provider, service and destination. GitHub describes its participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and UK Extension. Cloudflare and Spaceship describe contractual safeguards for relevant transfers, including standard contractual clauses and UK provisions. You can ask us for information about safeguards for a particular transfer.

See the GitHub privacy statement, Cloudflare data processing addendum, Spaceship data processing addendum, Apple privacy policy and DVSA personal information charter.

Why we use personal information

Where we need personal information to deliver the service you ask us to provide, such as an optional vehicle lookup, verifying Pro access or resolving a problem with your purchase, our basis is performance of our contract with you. Information needed for an optional feature is only required if you use it.

We rely on legitimate interests to answer general enquiries, moderate contributions, keep listings accurate, diagnose faults and protect the service against misuse. We use information to comply with a legal obligation where one applies, for example in handling a data-rights request. Device permissions let you control individual features. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Trip suggestions are planning aids that you can change. We do not use your information to make solely automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

How long records are kept

Local trip records, private cloud copies and exported files remain under the controls described above. The lookup service processes a registration for the lookup. Provider security logs have separate retention arrangements.

For support and complaints, the relevant factors are whether the issue is open, whether follow-up is needed, and whether particular records are needed for a dispute or legal obligation. For community records, a listing can remain useful while the place is current. Submissions and reports have no automatic expiry in the app. Taking down a public listing does not by itself erase the original moderation record. Email us to request review or deletion of either.

Your rights and how to complain

Email hello@roamulus.com to ask Lewis Gale, the controller, for access, correction, deletion or restriction, or for a portable copy where that right applies. You can also ask where we obtained information about you, who received it and how it was used. If someone submitted a location or detail about you, tell us which listing you mean. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request. A location or link is normally more useful than an identity document.

You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation. We will consider your circumstances and explain the outcome. Some information may need to be retained by law or for legal claims. We usually respond to rights requests within one month and will explain any lawful extension.

You can also complain directly to us by email. We will acknowledge a UK data protection complaint within 30 days, investigate and explain the outcome without undue delay. You can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or, in the EEA, your local data protection authority.

We will date revisions on this page and bring material changes to your attention as appropriate.
