Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. This policy covers the Roamulus app for iPhone and iPad (which replaces Vanlife Companion) and this website, operated by Lewis Gale. For privacy questions, contact hello@roamulus.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your entered profile name, an optional profile photo you choose (kept on this device only, not synced or shared), trips, stops, saved places, packing items, stock and shopping lists, expenses, vehicle records, renewal dates, notes, finished-trip milestones, manual resource readings and preferences locally. We do not run an account system or a server that receives these records. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also kept in your iCloud (see below). Roamulus has no account system and does not include advertising, behavioural analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs. Emergency numbers are included with the app, so looking one up sends nothing anywhere.

Data remains until you change or remove it, restore a replacement backup, or remove the app’s local data. Device backups and any backup provider you choose are controlled by your device settings and that provider. Deleting the app can remove its records.

Location, search and weather

With permission, the app uses your location while in use to find nearby places, request local weather or calculate road distances. It does not continuously track you in the background. Weather requests use coordinates rounded to roughly one kilometre. Trip-stop weather uses that stop’s saved coordinates.

Apple Maps receives search terms, map-area requests and route coordinates needed for its services. Apple Weather receives approximate weather coordinates. Weather and search results may be cached temporarily to reduce requests. You can disable location permission in iOS Settings, and disable local weather in the weather sheet. Place-name searches can be used without sharing your current location.

Campsites, facilities and overnight stops

Campsites, facilities (such as water, toilets, waste disposal, showers and laundrettes) and motorhome overnight stops come from OpenStreetMap and OpenCampingMap data included with the app, and some place names and touring data come from GeoNames. Searching them happens on your device and does not send your location or map area to us or to those projects.

Moving from Vanlife Companion

Roamulus can restore a backup file saved in Vanlife Companion. The file is read on your device and is not sent to us. If you used iCloud sync in Vanlife Companion, Roamulus uses the same private iCloud database, so turning on sync in Roamulus brings those records across. Nothing about your Vanlife Companion records is sent to us either way.

Trip diary photos and the camera

If you add photos to a trip diary, the app asks for access to your photo library. It keeps a reference to each photo you choose (not a copy) and, with your permission, groups them in an album named after the trip inside a Roamulus folder in the Photos app. Photos are never moved, uploaded or sent to us. Removing a photo from the diary takes it out of the album; it stays in your library. If you take a photo with the camera from the diary, it is saved to your photo library. You can change photo and camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

If you use iCloud sync or sharing, the diary keeps only the photo references, so photos themselves don’t appear on other devices or for a travel partner.

iCloud sync and sharing

Sync is off unless you turn it on (it is part of Pro). When it is on, a copy of the app’s records (trips, stops, expenses, diary notes, saved places, packing lists, stock and shopping lists, and van records, including renewal dates and your first name) is stored in your own private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can use it. We do not run a server that receives these records and we cannot read them.

If you invite a travel partner, the people you invite through Apple’s sharing screen can see and edit those same records, and Apple shows each of you the other’s name or contact details used for the invitation. You can stop sharing, remove people, or remove your records from iCloud in Profile → iCloud Sync & Sharing; a partner can stop using shared records in the same place. Each device keeps its own copy after sync or sharing stops. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Overnight spots suggested by travellers

If you choose to suggest an overnight spot, the details you enter (its map location, name or description, type, price, maximum stay, facilities and notes) are stored in the app’s public database on Apple’s iCloud. Suggestions are linked to an anonymous iCloud user identifier, not your name or email address, and we cannot see your Apple ID. Suggestions are reviewed before they are published; approved spots are visible to all users of the app. If you report a spot, the reason you choose is stored in the same way.

Suggesting or reporting requires you to be signed in to iCloud. Please don’t include personal details in notes. To ask for a suggestion you made to be removed, email us with the spot’s name and rough location. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Website icons

Place cards may request an icon directly from the place’s website. That website can receive your IP address and standard network request information; the icon request does not include your trip coordinates. Apple’s native place details and external websites are provided by their respective operators.

Vehicle registration lookup

If you choose to look up your van by its UK registration, the registration is sent to our vehicle lookup service, which requests that vehicle’s details from the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) MOT history service. The app receives the make, model, year of manufacture, engine size, fuel type, colour, current MOT expiry date and the most recent odometer reading recorded at an MOT. No other MOT history, and no other app data, is sent or received.

You choose whether to save these details, and you can switch off saving the MOT date and mileage before applying them. Saved details stay on your device like your other vehicle records. Our lookup service runs on Cloudflare Workers, so Cloudflare processes the request, including your IP address, to deliver it and to limit abuse. Our lookup service does not keep a record of registrations searched or results returned, and its request logging is switched off. DVSA and Cloudflare process requests under their own policies.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases, payments, subscription management and restores. The app uses Apple-verified transaction information to determine Pro access. We do not receive your card or bank details. Apple’s handling of account and payment information is covered by its own privacy policy.

Exports, navigation and contacting support

When you choose to export or share, the selected destination receives the content you choose to send. Navigation handoff shares a destination with your chosen Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze app. Review the recipient and content before sharing.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include, and use it to respond and resolve your request. Avoid sending sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website

This is a static website with no forms, advertising, analytics or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses and standard request information to serve and secure the site, under its own privacy statement. Email links open your own email application.

Your choices and questions

You can edit or remove local records, manage permissions in device Settings and choose whether to export backups or use optional online services. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support, email hello@roamulus.com.

Third-party services have their own policies, including Apple, OpenStreetMap, GeoNames, DVSA and Cloudflare. We will update this page if the app’s data practices change.
