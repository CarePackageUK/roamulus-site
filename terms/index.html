Who provides Roamulus and which terms apply

Lewis Gale provides Roamulus and operates this website. For product questions, a complaint or a problem with a purchase, email hello@roamulus.com.

The Apple Standard End User Licence Agreement governs the licence for the iPhone app. Apple’s purchase terms and the details shown at checkout apply to App Store transactions. This page explains how Roamulus works and how to get help. Your mandatory consumer rights continue to apply.

Purchases, trials and cancellation

Roamulus has free features and optional Pro purchases. Check the price in your currency, billing period, features and any trial or offer in Apple’s purchase sheet before confirming. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew until cancelled. If you accept a free trial, the displayed subscription price applies after the trial unless you cancel in time. Apple recommends cancelling a free or discounted trial at least 24 hours before it ends.

To change or cancel a subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Lifetime Pro is a one-off purchase. Buying it does not cancel an existing subscription. To restore a purchase for Roamulus, use the Apple Account that made it and choose Restore Purchases on the Pro screen.

To request a refund from Apple, visit Apple purchase support. You may also contact us about a fault or a charge. Eligibility for a refund or a statutory remedy depends on the purchase and the law where you live.

Consumers in the UK and EU generally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. For digital content supplied immediately, the right can end once supply begins if you expressly agreed to immediate supply, acknowledged the loss of the right and received the required confirmation. Check the details and instructions in your Apple receipt. Stopping renewal and exercising a statutory right to withdraw are separate steps. A fault with digital content can still give you a remedy.

Before you set off

Roamulus helps you plan a trip. Distances, timings, costs, weather, facilities and vehicle details are estimates or records from the sources shown. Check current road signs, height and weight restrictions, ferry schedules, site rules, opening dates and the operator’s instructions. A map pin or community listing does not grant permission to stay. Confirm that your vehicle can reach the site and that you may stay there.

A Schengen day count, where available, depends on the travel history entered and your circumstances. Check your dates, eligibility and visa or residence rules with the relevant immigration authority. Weather and renewal reminders can be delayed or unavailable. Check official warnings and documents when you need to make a safety or legal decision.

Set up navigation while safely parked or ask a passenger. For an emergency, contact the local emergency services directly. Network coverage and the ability to place a call depend on your device and location.

Sharing a spot or reporting a problem

Submit accurate information you have permission to share. Keep private addresses, third-party membership listings and personal information about other people out of submissions. When you choose to submit a spot, you ask us to review it and, if approved, display its details in Roamulus. You keep any rights you have in your contribution.

Use Report a problem on a listed spot, or email the location, a link if available, and the reason for your concern. A landowner, rights holder or traveller can also contact us directly. We can correct or remove inaccurate, unsafe, unlawful or rights-infringing material. Approved entries can become outdated between reviews.

Your files and third-party material

You control which trips, shopping lists and exports you share. A recipient may keep an independent copy. Use the app’s backup feature to save records you want to keep. Our privacy policy explains where the information goes and how to remove it.

Campsite and facility data includes material from OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database Licence and OpenCampingMap. Some place names and touring data come from GeoNames. The original licences continue to apply to the datasets. Apple Maps, Apple Weather and third-party sites have their own terms and attributions.

If something goes wrong

If a paid feature fails or digital content differs from its description, email us with the feature, your app version and what happened. Avoid sending payment card details or a whole trip backup. We can investigate and explain the remedies available.

UK consumers have statutory rights for paid digital content, including that it must be of satisfactory quality, fit for its purpose and as described. Depending on the circumstances, remedies can include repair, replacement or a price reduction. Consumers in other countries retain the mandatory protections and remedies that apply to them. See the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
