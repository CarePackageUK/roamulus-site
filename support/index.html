Vanlife Companion is now Roamulus. Same maker, new name, and a lot more road. Roamulus is a new app on the App Store, so here’s how to bring your trips with you.

Moving from Vanlife Companion

Bring your trips across with a backup

In Vanlife Companion, open Profile → Backup & Restore and save a backup file somewhere you can reach, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Then open Roamulus, go to Profile → Backup & Restore and restore that file. Roamulus reads Vanlife Companion backups as they are. Restoring replaces what’s in Roamulus, so do it before you start planning new trips there.

I used iCloud sync in Vanlife Companion

Roamulus uses the same private iCloud storage, so your synced trips appear when you turn on sync in Profile → iCloud Sync & Sharing. Sync is part of Roamulus Pro. If you don’t have Pro, the backup above works for everyone.

Does my Vanlife Companion Pro carry over?

Not automatically. Apple treats Roamulus as a new app, so purchases can’t move between them. If you bought Pro in Vanlife Companion, email us and we’ll help.

What happens to Vanlife Companion?

It’s being taken off sale, but if you have it you can keep using it. New features and fixes go into Roamulus.

Trips and Plan it for me

Plan a trip yourself

Start a trip, say where you’re heading, then add stops by place name, address or postcode. The + button on every trip adds a stop, a payment, a diary entry or something to buy. Free plans one trip at a time, and every trip you finish is kept as a milestone.

Let Roamulus plan it for you Pro

Start a trip and choose Plan it for me. Pick a destination, a region or a whole country, one way or a round trip, and how long you like to drive each day. Tell it if children or a dog are coming. Roamulus splits the journey into days, adds a campsite for every night and suggests places to visit. Don’t fancy a campsite? Swap it for another nearby. Check the plan before you set off.

Ferries and Eurotunnel Pro

When you plan a trip across the Channel, pick your crossing, or leave it blank and Plan it for me chooses one. Stops are kept on the right side of the water, and what you paid goes into your trip budget. Always check sailing times with the operator.

Trip costs and expenses

Open a trip’s Money section to set a budget by category. With Pro, log expenses from any stop and see them against your budget.

Campsites and maps

Find water, showers or somewhere to stay

Open Explore and search an area. You’ll find campsites, overnight stops, fresh water, toilets, showers, waste disposal, fuel, supermarkets and launderettes, plus garages, vets, pharmacies and hospitals. With Pro, filter overnight stops by what they offer. Details come from OpenStreetMap and the community, so always check access, facilities and overnight rules with the site.

Navigation, offline use and estimates

Open any stop in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. Searches, maps and weather need a connection, so download offline maps in Apple Maps before you go if you’ll be out of signal. Your saved trips and van records work offline. Drive times, distances, fuel and costs are estimates: check that roads suit your vehicle.

Today on the road

What Home shows while you’re away

During a trip, Home leads with today: your next stop and drive, tonight’s campsite, any crossing, the weather and what’s on your shopping list. Lupa keeps watch overnight, too.

Weather for every stop

Each stop shows the forecast for the day you’ll be there. Severe weather on your route shows on Home, windy crossing days are flagged, and on rainy days you can find indoor places nearby. Weather needs a connection.

Your van and renewals

Renewal dates and reminders

Add your MOT, insurance and road tax dates in My Van, and Roamulus counts down and nudges you before anything’s due. You can look up a UK van by its registration to fill in its details and MOT date. Check dates against your own documents.

Levels, stock and the shopping list

Record water, waste, gas and battery levels in My Van, and keep track of what you use up. When something runs low it goes on your shopping list. With Pro, share one list with your travel partner. Change units and currency in Profile.

Emergency numbers

Find the right number in any country

Police, ambulance and fire numbers for over 240 countries are saved in the app, with search, so they work without data. Tap a number to call. They’re free for everyone, always. If you’re ever unsure, 112 works across Europe and on many mobile networks elsewhere.

Backups and iCloud

Move to a new phone with a backup

Open Profile → Backup & Restore on your old phone and save a backup somewhere you can reach from the new one. Open the same screen on the new phone and restore it. Restoring replaces the app’s records after you confirm. Keep backup files private.

Sync devices or share a trip Pro

Open Profile → iCloud Sync & Sharing to turn on sync, and sign in to the same Apple Account on your own devices. To share a trip, choose it and invite your travel partner from the sharing screen. They join for free. Manage who has access from the same screen.

Pro and purchases

What’s in Free and Pro?

Free gives you Explore, your van, emergency numbers, one trip at a time and every trip you’ve ever taken. Pro adds Plan it for me, unlimited trips, overnight facility filters, sharing with your partner, expenses, reusable packing lists, more than one van, trip reminders, exports and iCloud sync.

Restore, manage or cancel Pro

Use the Apple Account you bought with, and open Roamulus Pro → Restore Purchases in Profile. Apple shows the plans and local prices before you buy. Subscriptions renew until cancelled in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Lifetime Pro is a one-off purchase, and buying it doesn’t cancel an existing subscription.

For billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support.

Still need a hand?

Tell us your app version, your device and what you were doing when the problem happened. Keep passwords, verification codes, payment details and private backups out of your email.
