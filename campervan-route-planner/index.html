Planning

Campervan Route Planner

Plan a campervan route with multiple stops, estimate driving time and fuel, and check campsite facilities before setting off.

Build the route around overnight stops

Start with your departure and overnight stops. Add places by name, address or postcode, then put fuel, food and sightseeing stops between them. Keep the longest days flexible so delays don’t force a late campsite arrival.

Estimate the drive and fuel

Use the app’s route estimate for road distance and driving time. Enter fuel details for your own van, or try the campervan fuel calculator with a distance and UK mpg before you download.

Check the pitch before you leave

Use Explore to look for campsites with showers, drinking water or waste disposal. Check what’s available, whether you need to book and whether your van fits. Facility details come from OpenStreetMap and the community, and coverage varies.

Example: Fort William to Mallaig

Add Glenfinnan as a stop on the roughly 45-mile sample trip. At 28 UK mpg, allow about 7.3 litres for the driving distance.

The app estimates the journey and can hand directions to Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. Check road widths, height restrictions and vehicle access before driving.
